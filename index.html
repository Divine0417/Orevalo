The Problem

Students are losing opportunities they never even heard of.

Every year, thousands of African students miss out on scholarships, internships, and jobs — not because they aren't qualified, but because the information is buried across dozens of websites.

😩Hours wasted searching scattered websites for scholarships with no clear results.
📄CVs and cover letters that don't stand out because there's no guidance.
📚No affordable AI tutor to help when studying gets hard.
🔗Career advice that's generic, not built for the African context.
⏱️Deadlines missed because opportunity alerts don't exist in one place.
🌍Brilliant students held back by lack of access, not lack of ability.
What Orevalo Does

Everything a student needs, finally together.

Six core tools designed around the real challenges African students face — from studying to landing their first opportunity.

🤖

AI Chat Tutor

Ask any academic question in plain language. Get clear, honest explanations — like having a patient tutor available 24/7.

🎓

Scholarship Finder

Browse a curated, regularly updated database of scholarships, fellowships, and grants open to African students — filtered to what matches you.

💼

Internship & Job Board

Discover internships, graduate jobs, and entry-level opportunities updated daily. No more checking five platforms at once.

📄

CV Builder

Create a professional, ATS-friendly CV in minutes using templates built for African graduates applying locally and internationally.

✉️

Cover Letter Generator

Write tailored cover letters for each application. Tell Orevalo the role and your background — it handles the words.

🔔

Opportunity Alerts

Get notified when new scholarships, internships, competitions, and grants match your profile. Never miss a deadline again.
