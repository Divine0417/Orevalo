# Orevalo Pricing Strategy

## Decision

Launch Orevalo free while the core opportunity experience is being validated. Do not charge for features that are not live yet.

The first paid offer should be a founding plan at NGN 1,000 per month after the product has demonstrated engagement. Move to the full Premium price of NGN 2,000 per month when paid features create clear, repeatable value.

The research signal is positive: 83% of 12+ respondents said they would pay something, with a target range of NGN 1,000 to 3,000 per month. This is an encouraging signal, not a market-wide forecast.

## Launch stages

| Stage | Price | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free launch | NGN 0 | Build trust and measure registrations, saves, return visits, and applications |
| Founding plan | NGN 1,000/month | Test willingness to pay with early users once paid features are useful |
| Premium | NGN 2,000/month | Fund the mature feature set after CV tools, alerts, and AI features are live |

Annual Premium can be offered at approximately NGN 20,000 per year, giving two months free.

## What each plan includes

### Free

- Browse internships and scholarships
- Basic account and profile
- Save a limited number of opportunities
- Apply through the original provider links

### Founding plan

Introduce this only after users have a reason to pay. The initial value should be:

- Unlimited saved opportunities
- Application tracking
- Personalised opportunity alerts
- Deadline reminders
- Founding-member pricing and feedback access

### Premium

- Everything in Founding
- CV builder and export
- Tailored cover letters
- Advanced opportunity matching
- Unlimited AI tutor access when the tutor is live
- Exclusive opportunities where available

## Pricing principles

1. Keep browsing the board and scholarship finder free. Discovery is the growth loop.
2. Charge for saved time, personalisation, reminders, and career outcomes, not basic access.
3. Never advertise a feature as included before it is available.
4. Use usage data to decide when to introduce payment: registrations, active users, saves, applications, return visits, and alert engagement.
5. Keep a meaningful free tier permanently.
6. Treat the 12+ respondent survey as directional evidence and re-test pricing with active users.
7. Explore university licensing later so institutions can sponsor Premium access for enrolled students.

## Current implementation rule

The current application should present the product as free during launch. Paystack and subscription controls should be added only when the paid feature set and billing terms are ready. Until then, pricing cards should label Founding and Premium as planned rather than implying that payment is available.
